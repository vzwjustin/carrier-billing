## 2024-05-18 - [Avoid Refactoring Array Methods on Small Arrays]
**Learning:** Refactoring standard functional array methods (e.g. `map().filter()`) into more verbose single-pass `for` loops on bounded or inherently small arrays (like phone line credits or device installments) is often dismissed as a micro-optimization with zero measurable benefit, sacrificing code readability unnecessarily.
**Action:** Only apply single-pass loop optimizations and avoid spread operators (e.g. `Math.max(...arr)`) when operating on arrays that can potentially scale up to large dynamic sizes (e.g., account-level or fleet-level datasets) where the performance impact or risk of `RangeError` is actualized.
